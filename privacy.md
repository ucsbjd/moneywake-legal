# Moneywake Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 7, 2026  **Last updated:** November 7, 2026

## 1. Who we are

Moneywake ("the app", "we", "us") is an independent information app for Android, operated by Core Apps Professional.
You can contact us at **coreappsprofessional@gmail.com**.

Moneywake shows public trade data from prediction markets and sends alerts about large trades. It does not accept
money for trading, hold funds, or place trades. It is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Polymarket.**

## 2. Where the information in the app comes from

* **Market and trade data.** Our servers fetch public trade and market data from Polymarket's public APIs and store a
  temporary copy that the app downloads.
* **Pictures and price charts.** The app loads market pictures, traders' profile pictures and a market's public price
  history directly from Polymarket (its public APIs and image hosting). These requests carry only what any web request
  does (your IP address and network details) and the item being asked for; no account, alert setting or other personal
  information is sent. Pictures are kept in the app's cache on your phone.
* **AI Insights.** Summaries are written by Google's Gemini model on our servers from that public trade data. **No
  personal information about you is sent to the model.**
* **Information about other people.** The app displays public, pseudonymous information that traders publish on
  Polymarket: usernames, profile pictures, wallet addresses (often shortened), leaderboard rank, profit and volume
  figures. We do not know who these people are and do not try to find out. See section 8 to ask us to remove or correct
  an entry.

## 3. Information we collect about you

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos,
precise location or other personal details. The app uses the following services, and each sends the data listed.

| What | Details | Why | Provided by |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Anonymous user ID** | A random ID created when you first open the app (Firebase Authentication, anonymous sign-in). It is not linked to your name or email. | To tell our server which installation has Full Access | Google (Firebase) |
| **Push token and alert settings** | A device token used to deliver notifications. Your alert filters (category, price range, trader group and minimum trade size), the traders you follow and the markets you watch are sent as notification-topic subscriptions. | To deliver the alerts you asked for | Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) |
| **Purchase information** | If you buy Full Access, Google Play handles the payment; we never see your card or bank details. We store an **order ID and purchase token** with your anonymous user ID and the time it was verified. If a Google Play reviewer unlocks Full Access with a review code, we store that the anonymous user ID was unlocked this way. | To confirm the purchase with Google, unlock Full Access, and restore it if you reinstall | Google Play; stored in Google Cloud Firestore |
| **Usage analytics** | App instance ID, device and app version, the app-open event and automatic events such as sessions, screen views and in-app purchases, and coarse location derived from your IP address | To understand how the app is used and improve it | Google (Firebase / Google Analytics) |
| **Crash reports** | Stack traces, app state and device details at the time of a crash, and an installation ID | To find and fix crashes | Google (Firebase Crashlytics) |
| **App integrity check** | A device and app attestation token | To protect our servers from tampered or fake apps | Google (Firebase App Check / Play Integrity) |
| **Server logs** | Requests to our servers include your IP address, which Google's infrastructure logs | Security and reliability | Google Cloud |

**We do not show ads.** We do not collect your Android advertising ID. We do not sell your personal information and we do
not use it for advertising. We share it only with the service providers above, who process it on our behalf.

## 4. What stays on your device

The app stores the following **only on your phone**: your alert profiles, follows, watches and display settings; onboarding
progress and a launch counter; a cached Full Access status; the alerts you received over the last year, with the trader names
and shortened wallet addresses shown on them, and their results; and the last AI Insights. You can clear the alert list from
the Alerts screen, or delete everything by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage. Android's automatic backup may copy
the alert list and results (not your settings) to your Google account backup; you control backup in Android settings.

## 5. How long we keep information

* Trade and market data copies on our servers: about **48 hours**; market results used for alert results: up to a few months.
* Purchase and review-code records (order ID, purchase token, anonymous user ID): until you ask us to delete them, or as long
  as the law requires us to keep records of a sale.
* Analytics: no longer than the Google Analytics data retention setting, which is at most 14 months.
* Crash reports: about 90 days (Firebase Crashlytics' standard retention).
* Server logs: about 30 days (Google Cloud Logging's standard retention).

## 6. Your choices and rights

* **Notifications:** turn them off in Android Settings, or change or switch off your alert profiles in the app at any time.
* **Delete your data:** email **coreappsprofessional@gmail.com**. If you bought Full Access, include your Google Play order ID
  (in your Google Play purchase history), which is how we find your record. We will delete the records we hold about you within
  **30 days**, except records we must keep by law.
* **Your rights.** Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what we hold about you, correct it, delete it,
  receive a copy, or object to its use. Email us to make a request; we will not treat you differently for doing so.

## 7. Where information is processed

Our servers and the services above run on Google Cloud and Firebase in the United States (Cloud Functions and Cloud Firestore
in us-central1). Moneywake is currently offered in the United States.

## 8. Information about other people shown in the app

Usernames, profile pictures, wallet addresses, rank, profit and volume of Polymarket traders are public. If you are one of them
and want an entry hidden from the app or corrected, email **coreappsprofessional@gmail.com** with the wallet address. We will
respond within 30 days. We cannot change the source data on Polymarket.

## 9. Links to other sites

Tapping a market or a trader in the app can open a page on polymarket.com in your browser. That site is not ours, we are not
affiliated with it, and its own privacy policy and terms apply once you leave the app.

## 10. Children

Moneywake is for adults. It is not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you
believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will delete what we hold.

## 11. Security

Connections to our servers and to Google's services are encrypted in transit (TLS). Our database rules let each installation read
only its own purchase record. No system is perfectly secure.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
